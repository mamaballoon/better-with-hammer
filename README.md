# Better With Hammer
- This mod adds two hammers to BTA!: the Iron Hammer and the Steel Hammer. The Iron Hammer breaks blocks down, turning one block into another, for decorative purposes. The Steel Hammer destroys them completely, leaving an amount of items in their place.

# Recipes:
   # Iron Hammer:
   - Every cobbled stone from every stone
   - Gravel from cobbled stone
   - Block of sulfur from hardened block of sulfur
   - Dirt from grass
   # Steel Hammer:
   - Pebbles from cobbled stone
   - Pebbles from dirt
   - Flint from gravel
   - Nethercoal from magma
   - Sand from sandstone
   - Brimsand from brimstone
   - Sulfur from sulfur blocks
